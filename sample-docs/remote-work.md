# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid model

Employees work in the office at least 2 days per week and from home on the other days. Teams agree on common office days so that meetings and pair programming can happen in person. Fully remote contracts exist only for roles approved by the Head of Engineering.

## Equipment

Each employee receives a laptop, a monitor and a headset. A one-time home-office stipend of 500 EUR covers a desk, a chair or similar furniture and is paid after the receipt is uploaded. Equipment stays the property of the company and must be returned at the end of the contract.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with the line manager's written approval at least 14 days in advance. Longer stays can create tax and social-security obligations for the company and need approval from the Finance team.

## Core hours

Everyone is reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local time. Outside these core hours, schedules are flexible as long as the agreed team commitments are met. On-call engineers receive a separate rota.

## Network security while remote

Company laptops must connect through the corporate VPN when using public Wi-Fi, for example in cafes or airports. Work documents must not be stored on personal devices or in personal cloud accounts.
