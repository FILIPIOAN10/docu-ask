# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, so an employee who joins in July receives 12.5 days for the first year. Requests are submitted in the HR portal and approved by the line manager, who answers within three working days.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year and must be taken before 31 March. Days above that limit expire without payment. The only exception is leave that could not be taken because of long illness, which is carried over in full.

## Sick leave

An employee who is ill must tell their manager before the start of the working day. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of absence. Sick leave is paid in full for up to 10 days per year; after that the public health insurance fund pays the statutory benefit.

## Parental leave

Mothers are entitled to 126 days of maternity leave. Fathers and partners are entitled to 10 working days of paternity leave, to be taken within the first two months after the birth. Either parent can additionally request parental leave of up to 24 months, unpaid by the company.

## Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays of the country where the employee's contract is based. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no compensatory day is granted.
