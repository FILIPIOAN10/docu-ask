# Travel and Expenses

## Booking travel

Business travel is booked through the travel portal. Flights shorter than 4 hours are booked in economy class; longer flights may be booked in premium economy. Train is preferred over flying for trips under 6 hours. Hotels are capped at 150 EUR per night in capital cities and 110 EUR elsewhere.

## Daily allowance

For every full day of business travel the employee receives a per diem of 35 EUR for meals, paid with the monthly payroll. Meals that are paid for by the client or by the conference are deducted from the allowance.

## Receipts

All expenses require a receipt, uploaded to the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses without a receipt are not reimbursed. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

## Approval limits

A single expense up to 200 EUR is approved by the line manager. Between 200 and 2,000 EUR the department director must approve. Anything above 2,000 EUR requires the CFO and must be approved before the money is spent.

## Not reimbursed

Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment and upgrades chosen for comfort are not reimbursed. Mileage with a private car is reimbursed at 0.25 EUR per kilometre.
