# Onboarding Guide

## First week

On the first day the new colleague receives the laptop, accounts and a welcome meeting with the team lead. A buddy from the same team is assigned and meets the newcomer daily during the first week. By Friday, the newcomer should have deployed a small change to the test environment.

## Probation

The probation period is 3 months. A feedback conversation takes place after 6 weeks and a decision meeting after 12 weeks. During probation the notice period is 5 working days.

## Mandatory trainings

Security awareness and data protection trainings must be completed within the first 30 days. Engineers also complete the secure coding course in the first quarter. Completion is tracked in the learning platform.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a personal learning budget of 1,000 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences, plus 3 paid learning days. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Benefits

The company pays private health insurance after the probation period, meal vouchers worth 8 EUR per working day, and a monthly gym contribution of up to 30 EUR. The benefits portal explains how to enrol family members.
